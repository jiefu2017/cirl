title: "Game Theory + Information Theory"
weight: 3
summary: "Using asymmetric information to enhance security and safety in robotic systems under adversarial conditions, and managing bidirectional information flow for cooperative and competitive planning."
---

This research examines how to use asymmetric information to enhance security
and safety in robotic systems operating in adversarial conditions, as well as
how to manage bidirectional information flow for planning in both cooperative
and competitive scenarios.
