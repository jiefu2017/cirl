The **Control and Intelligent Robotics Lab (CIRL)**, led by Dr. Jie Fu in the
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida,
integrates control theory, machine learning, and formal methods to construct
robust and provably correct autonomous robots and cyber-physical systems with
a holistic dynamic and epistemic model of the world.

Our work spans preference-aware decision-making with human involvement,
learning-enabled adaptive control with temporal objectives, and game-theoretic
autonomous system design, with applications in human-autonomy shared control,
cyber-physical system security, and cybersecurity.
